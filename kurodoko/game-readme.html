KURODOKO

A Nikoli logic puzzle on a 6×6 grid. Some cells are clues showing a number: that number is the total count of cells visible in a straight line from the clue (counting itself) in all four cardinal directions until blocked by a black cell or the grid edge.

Rules

  1. Sight count. Each clue's number equals the count of cells (including itself) visible in unbroken horizontal + vertical sightlines from that cell, stopping at the nearest black cell or grid edge in each direction.
  2. No adjacent blacks. Two black cells never share an orthogonal edge.
  3. Connected whites. All non-black cells (including clue cells) form a single orthogonally-connected region.

Clue cells stay white — they can never be black. Any black-toggle input on a clue cell is silently ignored.

Controls

  • Arrows or HJKL — move the cursor.
  • Space — toggle white ↔ black on the cursor cell (noop on clue cells).
  • X — clear the cursor cell to white.
  • On the title screen, press any key to begin.

Visual conventions

  • Black cell: solid black fill.
  • White cell: off-white interior.
  • Clue cell: off-white with a centered number; stays white.
  • Cursor: 3-pixel inset outline (black on white cells, off-white on black cells).

The grid is labeled A–F across the columns and 1–6 down the rows.
