Lanternkeep

A 1-bit top-down stealth puzzle. Twelve hand-designed courtyards. Two-color palette (#0a0a0a black, #e8e4d4 off-white) — no other colors, no external assets, no runtime dependencies.

Deploy

Static-site bundle. Three files:

  • index.html — entry HTML, references bundle.js via a relative path.
  • bundle.js — single minified ES2020 bundle, no runtime deps.
  • README.html — this file.

To host: copy dist/ to any static-file host (GitHub Pages, S3, Netlify, plain nginx). Works under any subpath because index.html references bundle.js with no leading slash.

Controls

  • Title screen: press any key to begin (Space recommended).
  • WASD or arrow keys: step one tile at a time (no diagonals).
  • Space: toggle the lantern on/off. ON reveals the whole room but oil drains; OFF leaves only a 3-tile aura around the player.

Mechanics

  • Guards & vision cones: stepping into a cone while the lantern is ON triggers a brief blackout and respawn at the level entry tile. Lantern OFF is detection-immune. Cones stay visible even when the lantern is OFF — they glow.
  • Oil meter: five segments, bottom-left of the canvas. Drains while the lantern is ON. At zero the lantern is forced OFF and cannot be re-lit until refilled.
  • Refill wells: stepping onto a well restores oil to full. Wells reset on level transition and respawn.
  • Patrols: some guards walk fixed routes. Cones sweep; routes are deterministic.

Win condition

Step onto the exit tile of the twelfth and final courtyard. The engine flips state.won to true, locks input, and after a brief hold the win/credits screen renders.
