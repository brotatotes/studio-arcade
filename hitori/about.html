Hitori

Shade duplicates. Keep whites connected. No two blacks touch.

▶ Play Hitori

About

Classic Hitori on a 6×6 numbered grid. Shade cells black so that each value appears at most once per row and per column among the remaining white cells. No two black cells may share an edge, and all white cells must form one orthogonally-connected region. Unique solution verified at generation; lower tiers require no guessing.

How to play

Arrow keys or HJKL move the cursor. Press Space to toggle shading at the cursor (white ↔ black). Press X to clear shading. Solve when each value appears at most once per row and column among white cells, no two black cells are adjacent, and all white cells form one connected region.

Features

  • 6×6 grid, pre-printed numbers 1..6 with duplicates
  • Three constraints: row/column distinctness on whites, no-adjacent-blacks, white-connectivity
  • Tier-targeted generation (easy / medium / hard / expert)
  • Unique solution verified at generation
  • Strict two-color palette (#0a0a0a + #e8e4d4)
  • Vanilla TypeScript + esbuild, single bundle
