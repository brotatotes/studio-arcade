YAJILIN

A Nikoli logic puzzle on a 6×6 grid. Some cells are clues showing an arrow and a number: the number says how many black cells exist in the arrow's direction from the clue cell to the edge of the grid.

Goal

Fill every non-clue cell as either BLACK or part of a single closed LOOP.

Rules

  • Each arrow-clue's number = count of BLACK cells in the arrow's direction (clue cells along the way are not counted, but they don't block the sight line — it continues to the grid edge).
  • No two BLACK cells are orthogonally adjacent.
  • BLACK cells are NOT part of the loop.
  • Clue cells are NOT part of the loop.
  • All loop cells form a single closed cycle, each entered exactly once.

Controls

  • Arrows — move the cursor.
  • Space — toggle empty ↔ black on a non-clue cell. If the cell was a loop cell, Space clears it to empty first.
  • L — toggle empty ↔ loop on a non-clue cell. If the cell was black, L clears it to empty first.
  • X — clear the cursor cell to empty.
  • Any input on a clue cell is silently ignored.
  • On the title screen, press any key to begin.

Visual conventions

  • Black cell: solid black fill.
  • Loop cell: off-white with a small black dot in the center. The loop's connecting segments are drawn automatically between adjacent loop cells.
  • Clue cell: off-white with the number in the top half and the arrow in the bottom half.
  • Cursor: 3-pixel inset outline (black on light cells, off-white on black cells).

The grid is labeled A–F across the columns and 1–6 down the rows.
