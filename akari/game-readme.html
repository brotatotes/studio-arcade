Akari

A two-color 10×10 Akari (Light Up) puzzle. One puzzle per seed; every generated puzzle has a unique solution.

Rules

  • Place light bulbs on the white cells of a 10×10 grid.
  • Each bulb illuminates its entire row and column until a black cell or the grid edge blocks the light.
  • Every white cell must be illuminated by at least one bulb.
  • No bulb may illuminate another bulb (no two bulbs share an unblocked row/column segment).
  • A numbered black cell (digit 0–4) must have exactly that many bulbs in its four orthogonal neighbors.
  • You win when all three conditions are satisfied. A SOLVED panel appears on win.

Visual conventions

Strict two-color palette: black ink on an off-white background. Every glyph below is rendered with that palette only — no greys, no anti-aliasing.

Controls

  • Title screen: press any key to begin.
  • Arrow keys: move the cursor (clamps to the grid).
  • Space: toggle a bulb on the selected white cell.
  • X: toggle a "no-bulb" mark on the selected white cell (player annotation only; mutually exclusive with a bulb).
  • Other keys ignored. Numbered black cells are not editable.

URL parameters

  • ?seed=N — integer seed for puzzle generation (default 1).

What's verified

  • Generator correctness & uniqueness — 1000-puzzle deterministic verifier; 100% uniqueness on accepted puzzles; ≥40% acceptance rate.
  • Difficulty distribution — technique-tier classifier (direct propagation → 1-step trial → multi-step trial → backtracking); bars ≥35/30/20/10% easy/medium/hard/expert; 100% no-guess at hard and below.
  • Solve correctness — 100 seeds replayed end-to-end via Playwright (file://) and 10 seeds replayed over real HTTP serve; each reaches state.won === true.
  • Legibility — Bar B blind vision-judge over 5 seeds with mid-game screenshots (bulbs placed + no-bulb marks); judge restates rules, counts black cells (±3), counts numbered black cells (±2), identifies illuminated white cells in coord form, identifies UI affordances. Pass ≥4/5.
  • Fuzz robustness — 200 puzzles × 10,000 random keypresses each (2,000,000 keys total); 0 pageerrors, 0 console errors, 0 state violations.
  • Performance — render-bar frame time and FPS sampled across 5 seeds; bar = 60 FPS / 16.7 ms.

What's NOT verified

  • Touch / mobile input. Keyboard-driven only; no pointer-tap or touch gesture support and no verifier coverage of mobile browsers.
  • Accessibility / screen readers. Pure canvas render — no DOM grid, no ARIA, no keyboard focus indicators outside the in-canvas cursor.
  • Cross-browser parity. Verifiers run under Chromium only (Playwright default). Firefox / WebKit not exercised.
  • Save state. Refreshing the page resets the puzzle. ?seed=N is the only state-restoration mechanism.
  • Real-time rule-violation feedback. Deliberately omitted (palette cleanliness); only checkWin fires on a fully-placed board.
